Process 05 · Under the fallen tree

Under the Roots

Under the Roots

SCROLL ↓
File 010 · Under the Roots · The step

The camera
gets there first.

  1. Blocked at 18 mm · 20 cm off the snow · up 24° · locked

    The lens sits on the crust, a metre and a half from the lead mammoth’s leg. From there the animal does not fit: the frame cuts off its head and rump, and the boy is a small shape at the top. The roots he will need are already in the corner.

  2. Blocked at 35 mm · handheld · 60 cm from his face

    The opposite of the first shot: handheld, lying beside him on the animal’s back, close enough to see his breath. The longer lens pulls the next mammoth’s head up behind his shoulder. We hold until he looks down past the camera and cut on that look.

  3. First camera again · same position · locked

    Back to the first camera on purpose. Same crust, same leg, now sunk, so you know where you are and how far down it is. This shot was not in the layout. We added it after the first rough cut, where he jumped from the back to the ground.

  4. Blocked at 24 mm · 25 cm off the floor · level

    The camera is inside the shelter before he is, looking out through the opening at the herd’s legs. He is backlit and has to crawl to us. As he lowers himself onto his elbows the lens comes down with him, to the height of the tinder.

  5. Blocked at 28 mm · 15 cm off the floor · up 37° · locked for 33 seconds

    One setup for more than half the film. The lens sits a hand’s width above the bark and looks up at him, so the ember, his fingers and both eyes share one frame. Nothing cuts away, so the wait is as long for you as it is for him.

Process 06 · Behind the rail post

Bear Bell

Bear Bell

SCROLL ↓
File 011 · Bear Bell · Walking down

The camera is
hiding too.

  1. Blocked at 27 mm · handheld · 1.1–1.5 m up · walking behind her

    An ordinary hiking video: her back, her pack, the road. The first sound is only heard. She slows, stops and closes her hand on her bell to listen, and the phone drifts in close behind her.

  2. Blocked at 27 → 170 mm · 1.4 m up · level · handheld

    The one who films sees it first, in the road mirror at the bend. In the layout this is a push past her cheek into a 170 mm zoom; in the film it is a cut. The leg is only a reflection here.

  3. Blocked at 27 mm · behind her left shoulder · down from 1.5 m to 0.6 m

    Behind her ear, so the bend stays in frame. The leg lands where the great bell hung a moment before. The layout swings it round the rock corner; the take brought it down from above, and we kept it.

  4. Blocked at 27 mm · 3 cm off the road · up 84°

    The phone goes down on the wet road, lens up, to hide its light. The great bell hangs over them like a roof. Then a hand covers the lens for two seconds: near-black, and you only hear it.

  5. Blocked at 27 mm · 45 cm off the road · beside her at the post

    Low behind the rail post, at her height. The others come down the road one after another, bells on their ankles, and the nearest plants its foot on the road beside them.

  6. Blocked at 27 mm · 0.7–0.9 m up · close on her face

    Back on her face for her one long breath out. Her hand goes to the lamp and it comes back on. We cut down with her eyes to the bell in her fingers, where the film ends.
